## Setting notes (background, no quotations)
- Luke 23:38-43: only Luke records the repentant criminal; Matthew 27:44 and Mark 15:32 say both reviled. Inscription: Luke 23:38 "This is the King of the Jews." Jesus' last words to a person before the final cries; "Truly, I say to you" (amen); "today" (semeron) and Bengel on joining it to "you will be" (Bible Hub). "Paradise" from a Persian word for an enclosed park or garden (Ellicott, Bible Hub); in the Septuagint used for Eden (Genesis 2). Only use of paradise by Jesus in the Gospels (Pulpit/Plumptre).
- Textual note: Cambridge Bible says "Lord" is omitted in the best manuscripts, "Jesus" alone; "in thy kingdom" vs "into". Augustine as cited in Cambridge Bible: mercy prayed so that misery might pray.
- Hebrews 10:19-39: 8:1-10:18 the central argument done; 10:19 begins the exhortation after "therefore". Three "let us": draw near (22), hold fast (23), consider (24). "Day drawing near" (25). Warning (26-31) cf. Deuteronomy 17:6 two or three witnesses, Deuteronomy 32:35-36 vengeance. Former days (32-34): public exposure, prison, plundering. Habakkuk 2:3-4 in 37-38. Verse 39 "preserve their souls".
- Proverbs 27: 25:1 men of Hezekiah copied proverbs; chapters 25-29. Verse 1 James 4:13-15, Luke 12 rich fool (Pulpit). Verse 13 mirrors 20:16 (security for stranger).
