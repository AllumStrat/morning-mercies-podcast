# Outline (plan day 10-2, episode 12, target 3300-3800)
1. Cold open: "I am not." Three times John says what he is not; the king in Eccl 2 says "I withheld nothing."
2. Welcome: recall yesterday's "take what is given"; three readings.
3. Ecclesiastes 2:1-16 (~850): experiment of pleasure (1-3), great works (4-8), the verdict (9-11), wisdom vs folly and common fate (12-16). Quotes: Cambridge (ashes), Henry (learned/ignorant), Poole (one event). Hard question: is pleasure the problem? Application: hold pleasures as gifts.
4. John 1:19-28 (~1100, heart): setting (Jerusalem delegation, Bethany beyond Jordan, reading discussion Cambridge/Origen), three denials (Christ, Elijah, the Prophet; Henry), Isaiah 40:3 voice (Cambridge, Pulpit), baptism question, one among you whom you do not know (Cambridge), sandal. Chrysostom on motive. Greek: phone / hesteken. Hardest question: John denies being Elijah but Jesus says he is Elijah (Matt 11:14); answer via Henry/Gill: person vs spirit and power. Application: be a voice.
5. James 1:12-18 (~1000): crown of life, peirasmos trial/temptation, God not tempting (Henry, Gill, Spurgeon), exelkomenos/deleazomenos fishing images, conception and birth of sin, v17 Father of lights (Cambridge, Spurgeon), v18 same birth verb, firstfruits.
6. Close (~300): real connections: Eccl 2 grasping vs James 1:17 gift; John deflects; question; preview John 1:29-34, James 1:19-27, Eccl 2:17-26.
Thread from yesterday: take what is given; grass in the sun; the unsatisfied eye (not repeating scrolling).
