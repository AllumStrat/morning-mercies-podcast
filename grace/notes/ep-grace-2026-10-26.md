## Background
Luke 24: Luke 23:56 says the women rested on the Sabbath according to the commandment, so first chance is Sunday. They come with spices prepared (23:56), expecting a corpse. Joanna: wife of Chuza, Herod's household manager (Luke 8:3). Jesus foretold death and rising in Luke 9:22, 9:44, 18:31-33. Greek: orthros batheos (deep dawn), leros (nonsense; Ellicott: only use in NT), othonia (linen cloths), thaumazo (marvel). Verse 12 absent from a few manuscripts, Pulpit says genuine; John 20 parallels.
Hebrews 11:32-40: names Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel from Judges and Samuel. Stopped lions (Daniel 6), quenched fire (Daniel 3), women received dead (Zarephath 1 Kings 17, Shunammite 2 Kings 4, Cambridge/Barnes). Tortured, Greek tympanizo, beaten on a drum or wheel, 2 Maccabees 7 tradition (biblestudytools snippet). Better resurrection: kreitton, key word of Hebrews. teleioo perfect. Hebrews written to Christians under pressure, so the point of the list: faith endures when rescue does not come.
Proverbs 28: Hezekiah's men collection (25:1). 28:15 lion/bear, shepherd-predators (1 Sam 17:34).
