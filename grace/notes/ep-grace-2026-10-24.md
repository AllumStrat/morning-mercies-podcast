## Setting notes (general background, not quoted)
Luke 23:44-49: sixth to ninth hour = noon to three. Passover, full moon. Psalm 31:5 is the source of Jesus' prayer. Luke omits "My God, my God" cry. Greek paratithemai = to place alongside for safekeeping, deposit. Centurion in Luke glorifies God; says dikaios, righteous or innocent.
Hebrews 11: hypostasis (assurance, substance, foundation), elenchos (conviction, proof). Habakkuk 2:4 quoted at Hebrews 10:38.
Proverbs 27: Hezekiah's collection (25:1). 27:17 iron.
