## Setting notes
Eccl 2:17-26: Qoheleth, traditionally Solomon; heir = Rehoboam (1 Kings 12). Hebel = breath. 2:24-26 first of the "nothing better" refrains (3:12-13, 3:22, 5:18, 8:15, 9:7-10).
John 1:29: "next day" = third day of the testimony sequence (v19, v29, v35). Bethany beyond the Jordan (v28). airon = lift up/take away. amnos. hamartia singular. Isaiah 53, Exodus 12, Genesis 22:8, daily lamb Exodus 29. Spirit as dove, remained (menō).
James 1:19-27: tachys/bradys; logos emphytos "implanted word" (cf v18 word of truth); katanoeo mirror (bronze mirror, Pulpit); paralambanō/parakypsas stoops to look; nomos teleios tes eleutherias; thrēskeia.
