## Setting and background
Proverbs 29: chapters 25-29 are the second Solomonic collection copied by Hezekiah's men (Prov 25:1). Chapter 29 begins with the stiff neck and ends with the contrast of the unjust and the upright, which Pulpit Commentary calls a fitting close of the book's long collection. Verses 15-17 repeat the rod and reproof; 18 the vision; 19-22 servant, hasty speech, anger; 23-27 pride, thief, fear of man, ruler, final contrast.
Luke 24:28-35: the same first day of the week; the village is Emmaus (Luke 24:13, sixty stadia from Jerusalem); evening. The scene follows 24:13-27 where Jesus expounded Moses and all the Prophets. Peter's appearance is attested at 1 Corinthians 15:5 (ESV cross reference). Greek: parebiasanto "they urged strongly/constrained" (Meyer, MacLaren); kataklino "reclined at table"; prosepoieito "made as though" (Vincent, Meyer: no deceit).
Hebrews 12:14-29: part of the closing exhortation; the author pulls from Esau (Gen 25, 27), Sinai (Ex 19, Deut 4; Heb 12:20 cites Ex 19:12-13), Zion, Abel's blood (Gen 4:10), Haggai 2:6 (Heb 12:26), Deut 4:24 (Heb 12:29). Cambridge Bible says the consuming fire reference is to Deut 4:24.
Hard questions: (1) Luke: why were their eyes kept? Calvin: covered, not changed; Poole: the veil withdrawn; the sacramental reading of Augustine and Chrysostom reported by Calvin and Pulpit; Calvin rejects it. (2) Hebrews: Esau found no chance to repent: Chrysostom says his tears were of pride and wrath, not repentance.
